    隐私与安全Privacy and safety

    1. 订阅链接是凭据:不写日志,也只发给它自己的主机。Your subscription link is a credential: never logged, sent only to its own host.
    2. 链接在你的机器上转换,没有任何转换服务见过它。Links are converted on your machine, by no third-party service.
    3. 系统代理与 TUN 默认关闭,只在你打开时改动,退出时还原。System proxy and TUN are off by default and restored when Hop quits.
    4. 不读写其他 Clash 客户端的文件或进程。Reads and writes nothing of another Clash client.
    5. 所有数据只在本机,目录权限 0600。All data stays on your machine, mode 0600.
